About Leadra Labs

Leadership, built in real moments.

Leadership is shaped in the moments you cannot plan for. The quiet pause before a hard conversation. The hesitation before you speak up. The weeks where everything shifts and your team needs you to stay steady. These moments define how people experience you, yet most leaders face them alone, relying on instinct or whatever they remember from training long ago.

Leadra is a behavioural development app that supports you in those moments. It helps you slow down just enough to see what is really happening, understand your own patterns and choose a response that strengthens your leadership. Leadra gives you practical guidance you can use immediately, so your everyday moments become opportunities to grow rather than situations you simply try to get through.

Leadership development matters because the demands on leaders are higher than ever. Teams need direction. Organisations need momentum. People need leaders who can regulate themselves, think clearly, read situations well, adapt to change and influence others without creating friction. These five capabilities shape real leadership, and Leadra helps you build them through simple, repeatable habits that fit into real life.

Leadra was created in Australia for leaders at every stage of their journey. Emerging leaders finding their footing. Mid-career leaders navigating complexity. Senior and C-suite leaders carrying the weight of organisational impact. No matter where you sit, the moments that matter keep coming. Leadra helps you meet them well.

Meet the founder

NJ

Nova Jackson

Nova Jackson has spent more than fifteen years helping leaders grow across large and complex organisations in the UK and Australia. With a degree in applied psychology, she has built her career around understanding how people think, learn and lead, and how small behavioural shifts can create meaningful change in teams and organisations.

Her work spans the full spectrum of leadership development. She has designed and delivered learning and development programs based on a spectrum of adult learning principles, built leadership frameworks, shaped early-career pathways and led strategic talent initiatives and created pathways that support leaders from emerging through to executive levels. Nova's experience in organisational development and culture transformation has given her a deep understanding of how leaders influence the systems around them, and how those systems in turn shape behaviour.

She is trained in adult learning principles and has spent years coaching leaders and teams using tools like the Human Synergistics Group Styles Inventory (GSI) and Life Styles Inventory (LSI), helping them understand their patterns, strengthen their impact and build healthier team dynamics. Her work in team effectiveness, leadership coaching and behavioural change has made her a trusted partner for leaders navigating complexity, growth and organisational pressure.

Nova founded Leadra to bring all of this experience into a tool leaders can use every day. After years of watching leaders receive development only in workshops or annual programs where theory and models prevailed, she set out to create something more practical, tangible and human: a behavioural development app that supports leaders in the moments that matter, helping them build clarity, confidence and the capabilities that define genuine leadership.

How we use AI

Leadra uses AI models built by Anthropic to help you make sense of your reflections, and we want to be upfront about what that means. When you enter a journal entry, the AI looks for patterns in what you've written and offers a short, practical next step linked to one of the five core leadership capabilities: Emotional Regulation, Critical Thinking, Situational Judgement, Change Agility and Influence. Every micro-moment and summary is designed to strengthen one of these capabilities in a way that feels simple and immediately useful.

The AI is not a therapist, and it is not trying to act like one. It works more like a thoughtful mentor who reads your notes and offers a gentle nudge to help you see the moment more clearly. It does not diagnose, judge or make assumptions about your workplace or the people in it. Its only job is to reflect your experience back to you so you can grow from it.

Your reflections are yours. We do not sell your data, and the AI exists for one purpose: to help you strengthen the capabilities that make real leadership possible.
